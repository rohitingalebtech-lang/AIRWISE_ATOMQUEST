4. Synthetic Dataset Assumptions & Scientific Limitations

Transparent documentation of how the synthetic data was constructed.

Important Notice

1. Heat Index Formula Proxy

A mathematical proxy discomfort score S = Temp + 0.14 × (Humidity - 40) + Time_Offset was used to simulate the relationship between wet-bulb convective evaporation and ambient warmth.

2. Circadian Time Offsets

Metabolic and acoustic factors were approximated: Night (-2.0 comfort offset for quiet sleep), Morning (0.0 neutral baseline), Afternoon (+1.5 peak heat and activity), and Evening (+0.5 residual warmth).

3. Simulated Human Stochasticity

A 10% stochastic perturbation (±1 level) was injected into the synthetic labels to reflect empirical human comfort variance as documented in ASHRAE 55 thermal comfort standards.

4. Scientific Limitations

Synthetic data does not prove real-world human comfort. Real biological perception depends on clothing insulation (clo), metabolic rate (met), radiant temperatures, and air velocity. This software prototype proves algorithmic learning feasibility only.
