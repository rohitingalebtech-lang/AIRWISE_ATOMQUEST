3. Architectural Trade-Off Analysis

Decision Tree Strengths

  • Automatically models non-linear psychrometric curves (e.g. wet-bulb thermal stress).
  • Low computational footprint suitable for embedded microcontrollers (ESP32/ARM Cortex).
  • Highly interpretable via decision path inspection.

Rule-Based Limitations

  • Requires extensive manual tuning of edge-case rules.
  • Fails to capture subtle compound effects (e.g. moderate heat + extreme humidity).
  • Cannot personalize without compounding code complexity.
